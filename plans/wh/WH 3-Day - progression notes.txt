WH 3-Day — progression notes
============================

Companion to the WH 3-Day - <Day>.fnw plans (a .fnw has no notes field, so the
cues that don't fit as reps/weight live here). Generated by
scripts/programs/wh_3day.py.

Schedule
--------
Jujitsu only TWICE a week, Tuesday + Thursday. Three lifts total:

  Sun  RDL ↔ tibialis ↔ quad stretch  /  Leg press ↔ curl ↔ split squat ↔ hyper  /  Elephant walk
  Tue  Hip adduction ↔ abduction ↔ tibialis  /  Leg press ↔ curl ↔ hyper      (short, after JJ)
  Thu  Hip adduction ↔ abduction ↔ tibialis  /  Leg press ↔ curl ↔ hyper      (short, after JJ)

(↔ = superset.) Tue/Thu are SHORT sessions done right after the JJ class — you
shower at the gym anyway. Sunday is the one longer standalone day; it doesn't
have to be Sunday, use any non-JJ day (it's the freshest-back day, so the heavy
RDL lives there).

The hip circuit runs 6 rounds; the leg-press/curl/hyper circuit runs 4 rounds on
the short days; Sunday's leg superset runs 3 rounds (split squat round 1 is a
bodyweight on-ramp, then 2 working sets).

Low back is trained Sun/Tue/Thu — never on consecutive days (Mon, Wed, Fri+Sat
rest between). The heavy snatch-grip RDL lands on the fresh standalone day ONLY,
never on a post-JJ back; the hypers carry the low-back progression on the two
short days.

WH machines you don't have at home get the emphasis: the leg press (loaded for
glutes), the hip adduction/abduction machine, and the tibialis ("tip") machine.

Volume (why the set counts are what they are)
---------------------------------------------
A muscle needs >=12 sets/week (secondary muscles at 0.5, pooled across every
movement) to be progressed (add load OR reps). Below 12 is fine for maintenance.
Actual weekly totals from this plan:

  Progressing (>=12/wk): hamstrings 27, glutes 20, tibialis 15, low back 13,
    adductors 12, abductors 12.
  Maintenance: quadriceps 9.5 (split squat), calves 13 (carried by the leg-press
    and curl secondaries — no direct calf work; add a seated calf raise as a
    short-day filler if you want it direct).

The leg-press/curl/hyper block on the SHORT days is load-bearing for the rehab:
it's what keeps low back, glutes and hamstrings at their floors. If a Tue/Thu
session gets cut short, keep that block over the hip circuit — glutes/hams have
margin, but dropping it puts the low back under the floor.

Movement notes
--------------
- Leg press is for GLUTES: feet high, deep stretch. Standing in for squats until
  the back is strong enough to squat again. Load it up — you don't have one at
  home.
- Hamstring curl: single-leg machine, reps per side, starting 12/side @ 100.
  Logged as "Hamstring Curl" (same as last year, history carries over).
- Split squat: quad/knee MAINTENANCE, two big DBs at the sides (less low-back
  demand than a barbell front rack). Reps per side; weight = total of both DBs
  (two 45s = 90). Fresh day only — a balance/stability move stays off the tired
  post-JJ days. Round 1 is a bodyweight on-ramp, then 2 working sets.
- RDL: snatch-grip, NO straps (grip trained raw). Held steady at 155 while the
  hypers progress. Warm-up ramp: 2x empty bar x12, 95x12, 135x8 — logged as
  WARM-UP sets, so they add gym time but not working volume. Sunday only.
- Quad stretch (couch stretch): 2 min/side x2, riding the RDL rest to prep the
  split squats.
- Hip abduction is MAXED at the machine's 140 ceiling — progress by reps only.
  Adduction can still take load. 6 rounds each on Tue/Thu = 12/wk each.
- Tibialis progresses by reps (light fixed load): 3 on Sunday, 6 on each short
  day = 15/wk. You like the machine and don't have one at home — use it.
- Elephant walk: single set, Sunday finisher — posterior-chain decompression.

Hyperextension progression (the rehab centerpiece)
--------------------------------------------------
11 sets/week (Sun 3, Tue 4, Thu 4). With the RDL held steady, the hypers are the
low-back progression driver; 11 hyper sets plus the RDL's erectors put the low
back at ~13 (the tool counts the RDL's erectors at half).

The ladder (from Back_extension_progression.txt):
    1. 2 min hold
    2. 1 min hold each side, one leg
    3. 35 reps
    4. 20 reps each side, one leg
    5. Loaded reps (one or two legs)
