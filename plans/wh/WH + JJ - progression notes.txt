WH + JJ — progression notes
============================

Companion to the WH + JJ - <Day>.fnw plans (a .fnw has no notes field, so the
cues that don't fit as reps/weight live here). Generated by
scripts/programs/wh_jj.py.

Structure
---------
Five short lifts, Mon-Fri, done right after jujitsu; no weekend sessions.
JJ ~5x/week covers upper-body pulling/pressing/grip, so the lift is lower
body, posterior chain, and back rehab on WH's machines. (↔ = superset.)

  Mon  RDL ↔ tibialis ↔ quad stretch   /   Leg press ↔ curl ↔ split ↔ hyper
  Tue  Hip adduction ↔ abduction ↔ wrist rotation ↔ wrist extension   /   Calf ↔ tibialis ↔ ext rotation ↔ band neck
  Wed  Leg press ↔ curl ↔ split ↔ hyper
  Thu  Hip adduction ↔ abduction ↔ wrist rotation ↔ wrist extension   /   Calf ↔ tibialis ↔ ext rotation ↔ band neck
  Fri  RDL ↔ calf ↔ tibialis ↔ quad stretch   /   Leg press ↔ curl ↔ split ↔ hyper   /   Elephant walk

The leg superset runs 3 rounds (leg press/curl/hyper 3 each; split squat 1
bodyweight on-ramp + 2 working). The hip superset runs 6 rounds.

Low back is clustered on Mon/Wed/Fri and RESTED Tue/Thu (+ weekend) — never
trained on consecutive days. RDL lands Mon and Fri only: the freshest back days
(after the weekend, and before it), never on a pre-fatigued back.

JJ intensity: 5 days on the mat, but only ~2-3 hard-rolling days; the rest
technique / drilling / positional, so recovery holds up and the back stays
protected.

Volume rule (why the set counts are what they are)
--------------------------------------------------
A muscle needs >=12 sets/week (secondary muscles at 0.5, pooled across every
movement) to be progressed (add load OR reps). Below 12 is fine for maintenance.
3 rounds on Mon/Wed/Fri is enough because everything pools:

  Progressing (>=12/wk): glutes (~22), hamstrings (~27), low back (13),
    adductors (12), abductors (12), tibialis (12).
  Maintenance: quads (split squat), calves, rotator cuff.
  Wrist prehab: 12/week each side (rotation + extension).

Movement notes
--------------
- Leg press is for GLUTES: feet high, deep stretch. Standing in for squats
  until the back is strong enough to squat again.
- Single-leg hamstring curl REPLACES the Nordic curl (lower DOMS). Logged under
  "Hamstring Curl" (same exercise as last year, history carries over). Reps per
  side; starting 12/side @ 100.
- Split squat: quad/knee MAINTENANCE, two big DBs at the sides (less low-back
  demand than a barbell front rack). Reps per side; weight = total of both DBs
  (two 45s = 90). Round 1 each day is a bodyweight on-ramp, then 2 working sets.
- RDL: snatch-grip, NO straps (grip trained raw). Held steady at 155 while the
  hypers progress. Warm-up ramp: 2x empty bar x12, 95x12, 135x8 — logged as
  WARM-UP sets, so they add gym time but not working volume. Mon/Fri, with calf/
  tibialis + the quad stretch filling the rest.
- Quad stretch (couch stretch): 2 min/side x2, riding the RDL rest on Mon/Fri to
  prep the split squats.
- Hip abduction is MAXED at the machine's 140 ceiling — progress by reps only.
  Adduction can still take load.
- Tibialis progresses by reps (light fixed load); 3 sets on Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri = 12.
- Calves are MAINTENANCE (2 sets Tue/Thu/Fri = 6).
- Wrist prehab (rotation + extension) rides every round of the Tue/Thu hip
  superset — 12/week each. Anti-flexion (extensors, top of the forearm) +
  rotation balance the heavy gripping from JJ and raw-grip RDLs; keeps the
  wrists and elbows healthy.
- Rotator cuff: Cable External Rotation, 3 sets on Tue/Thu with the calf/tib
  block — shoulder-health maintenance for all the grappling pulls/posts.
- Band neck (flexion + extension): the biggest durability move for 5x/week
  grappling, on the Tue/Thu accessory block. NEW to you — start LIGHT, 2 sets
  each, and build slowly; the neck gets sore fast when it's untrained. Add
  lateral (side-to-side) work once flexion/extension feel solid, and if the gym
  has a neck machine you can swap the bands for it later.
- Elephant walk: single set, Friday finisher.

Hyperextension progression (the rehab centerpiece)
--------------------------------------------------
3 sets, in the leg superset, on Mon/Wed/Fri = 9 sets/week. With the RDL held
steady, the hypers are the low-back progression driver; 9 hyper sets plus the
RDL's erectors put the low back at ~13 (the tool counts the RDL's erectors at
half).

The ladder (from Back_extension_progression.txt):
    1. 2 min hold
    2. 1 min hold each side, one leg
    3. 35 reps
    4. 20 reps each side, one leg
    5. Loaded reps (one or two legs)
