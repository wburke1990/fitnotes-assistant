WH + JJ — progression notes
============================

Companion to the WH + JJ - <Day>.fnw plans (a .fnw has no notes field, so the
cues that don't fit as reps/weight live here). Generated by
scripts/programs/wh_jj.py.

Structure
---------
Five short lifts, Mon-Fri, done right after jujitsu; no weekend sessions.
JJ ~5x/week covers upper-body pulling/pressing/grip, so the lift is lower
body, posterior chain, and back rehab on WH's machines.

  Mon  Leg press + single-leg ham curl (superset) / split squat + tibialis / hyper
  Tue  RDL + tibialis (superset) / hip adduction + abduction (superset)
  Wed  = Mon
  Thu  hip adduction + abduction / calf + tibialis / core rehab
  Fri  RDL (heavy) + calf / leg press / hyper

JJ intensity: 5 days on the mat, but only ~2-3 hard-rolling days; the rest
technique / drilling / positional, so recovery holds up with no rest days and
the back stays protected.

Volume rule (why the set counts are what they are)
--------------------------------------------------
A muscle needs >=12 sets/week (secondary muscles counted at 0.5) to be
progressed (add load OR reps) without the injury risk of driving weight into
too little volume. Below 12 is fine for maintenance. Sets/session for a target
= 12 / weekly frequency.

  Progressing (>=12/wk): glutes (leg press), hamstrings (RDL + curl),
    adductors, abductors, tibialis, low back.
  Maintenance (<12/wk): quads (split squat), calves, core.

Movement notes
--------------
- Leg press is for GLUTES: feet high, deep stretch. Standing in for squats
  until the back is strong enough to squat again.
- Single-leg hamstring curl REPLACES the Nordic curl (lower DOMS, which matters
  with JJ every day). Logged under "Hamstring Curl" -- the same exercise used
  last year, so the history carries over; starts at last year's weight. The 4
  logged sets are per leg.
- RDL: snatch-grip, NO straps — grip is trained raw (JJ game isn't grip-heavy).
  Up +20 lb last mesocycle; now HELD STEADY at 155 both days (Tue and Fri) while
  the hyperextension progression is the focus. The calf next to Friday's RDL
  just fills the rest.
- Hip abduction is MAXED at the machine's 140 lb ceiling — progress it by reps
  only. Adduction can still take load.
- Tibialis progresses by reps (light fixed load), fed as the superset filler so
  the 12 sets cost almost no extra time.
- Split squat is quad/knee MAINTENANCE only — no load chasing.

Hyperextension progression (the rehab centerpiece)
--------------------------------------------------
3 sets, always the LAST movement of the session, with nothing spine-loading
after it — hypers leave the low back acutely weak. Spread to 3x/week
(Mon/Wed/Fri) = 9 sets. With the RDL held steady, the hypers are the low-back
progression driver; 9 hyper sets plus the RDL's erectors put the low back at ~13
sets (the tool counts the RDL's erectors at half, so it reads 13).

The ladder (from Back_extension_progression.txt):
    1. 2 min hold
    2. 1 min hold each side, one leg
    3. 35 reps
    4. 20 reps each side, one leg
    5. Loaded reps (one or two legs)
